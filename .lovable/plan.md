
# Ben Paul Richard - Engineering Portfolio Website

## Overview
A dark-themed, research-grade portfolio website with a professional academic/lab aesthetic. The site will feature scroll-triggered video playback, animated elements, and a sophisticated engineering-first design.

---

## Design System

### Theme & Colors
- **Dark theme** with deep navy/charcoal background
- Engineering-inspired accent colors (subtle cyan/electric blue)
- High contrast for readability

### Typography
- **Headings**: Modern monospace font (JetBrains Mono or Space Mono)
- **Body**: Times New Roman / Serif for academic feel
- **UI Accents**: Monospace for technical elements (metrics, labels)

### Visual Elements
- Subtle animated grid pattern (NOT on hero)
- Soft gradient overlays
- Minimal particle effects in background
- Engineering schematic-inspired decorative lines
